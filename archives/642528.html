纯理科纯英文笑话，中文翻译不出来那种

Posted 2009-09-15 | archive

A mathematician walks into a bar and asks for ten times the normal number of drinks anyone else has. "Wow," says the barkeep, "That's an order of magnitude."

order of magnitude 的意思是数量级

Two atoms walk into a bar.
One atom says, "You know, I think I forgot an electron..." The other atom asks, "Are you sure??" The first atom replies, "I'm positive!"

Reminds me of: A neutron walks into a bar and orders a beer. The bartender gives him a beer and the neutron asks "How Much?" The bartender replies "For you, no charge!"

What do you get when you cross a mosquito and a mountain climber?
You can't cross a vector with a scalar!

这里vector的意思是生物学里的“（传染疾病的）介体，载体”，例如Vector of disease；
scalar的意思是 one who scales (climbs) mountains.

再来个脑筋急转弯
Q: What's the value of a contour integral around Western Europe?
A: Zero, because all the Poles are in Eastern Europe.

Poles的意思是波兰人。
