开源项目的10条黄金定律

Posted 2008-04-15 | archive

  1. If you must criticize, criticize with a gentle and humble tone
    温和地批评
    1. Unless the evidence suggests otherwise, assume that users you don't know are not interested in committing to the project
      用户一般都不是很愿意贡献代码
    2. Despite the evidence, it's probably your fault
      大量出现的问题，很有可能是你自身的问题。有的时候FAQ和文档都在哪里，但是用户视而不见，会不会因为链接埋藏太深呢？
    3. Follow the rules you apply to those with lower karma!
      （说实话这句不知道怎么翻译好，karma是佛教用语 羯磨 的意思）设定好一个规则，并且虔诚地遵守
    4. Don't apply new rules casually, and simplify if possible
      尽量少建立新规则，如果必须的话，建立简单的规则
    5. Assume everything you ever say will become permanent and don't say things that will come back to haunt you
      对自己说的话负责任
    6. People like to improve their code, and like coding standards
      人们喜欢改进代码，特别是代码风格
    7. Assume there will be politics, and plan for them
      为“政治”内容准备一点
    8. You have two audiences: developers and users
      你有两个受众：用户和开发者
    9. Dream big, baby. Plan for the future
      为未来打算！
