Tutorial how to P2P watch Olympics for free legally.

Posted 2008-09-06 | archive

OK, I did not expect that watching Olympics in the US are that hard. First maybe in a capitalism country you can't watch anything freely, secondly P2P Live streaming technology are more popular & advanced in China? LOL

Just few steps. First download est's version (no ads, no spyware) of PPLive. This version is the earliest version of PPLive(aka Synacast, aka SynaLive), a well-known free P2P Live Streaming service in China. CCTV(CCTV here means China Central Television, not Closed-circuit television) give PPLive rights to broadcast Olympics. So it's totally legal. Too bad CCTV do not provide English commentators. :-(

Second, unpack, and run PE.exe. If you see Chinese characters jut right click the icon on your system tray, choose Languages - 1033 English

Next, copy paste these URL in your browser. If it doesn't work just restart your browser for syncast:// schema activation

CCTV-5 (Sports Channel & Olympics Channel) Hi-res

CCTV-1 Hi-res

CCTV-5 for Low-bandwidth

CCTV-1 for low-bandwidth

You can also choose a Channel from the Channels menu in PPLive instead of copy-paste these long URL's

Finally, when buffer is 100% complete, ignore everything PPLive says, open the streaming in VLCPlayer or Windows Media Player with the address:
http://127.0.0.1:8888/1.asf
or

or
http://127.0.0.1:8888/
or

or
http://your_IP_address :8888/1.asf for your LAN friends, yes you can install PPLive on one machine and streaming for whole LAN. If you open multipule channels in PPLive, just change the :8888 port to :8889. e.g. http://127.0.0.1:8889/

If you find est's version of PPLive un-trust worthy, just try download the newest official version of PPLive(with Ads of course), and open the installation folder look for /ChannelXML/Default.XML

One last thing, no "boycott XXX/Free XXX/XXX rights" shit here.
