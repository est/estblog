分享下我的Thunderbird自动分拣filter - 激活信篇

Posted 2007-09-08 | archive

用了1.5年的DreamMail，由于这个化石级软件长期不更新，加上我需要在Linux下用一个可以和Windows同步的邮件客户端，所以我选择了Thunderbird。

其实平时我们用Email很大一部分邮件是各个论坛、网站的注册激活信，我一般把这些信件放在 激活信 文件夹里。并设定了一个filter 过滤器，让Thunderbird自动捡信分类。

自动分检的原理很简单，只要邮件的标题包含下列关键词就分类到 激活信文件夹：

Verif validate 激活 验证 帐号 activate 欢迎 account confirm regist welcome to 账户 password invit 密码 sign up 注册 member

使用了Message Filter Import/Export插件的导出格式，需要的朋友直接保存下列文本为任意文件并执行 Tools -> Import Filters 即可：

[code:none]RootFolderUri=mailbox://est@pop3.126.split.netease.com
version="8"
logging="no"
name="激活信"
enabled="yes"
type="1"
action="Move to folder"
actionValue="RootFolderUri/%E6%BF%80%E6%B4%BB%E4%BF%A1"
condition="OR (subject,contains,Verif) OR (subject,contains,validate) OR (subject,contains,激活) OR (subject,contains,验证) OR (subject,contains,帐号) OR (subject,contains,activate) OR
(subject,contains,欢迎) OR (subject,contains,account) OR (subject,contains,confirm) OR (subject,contains,regist) OR (subject,contains,welcome to) OR (subject,contains,账户) OR (subject,contains,password)
OR (subject,contains,invit) OR (subject,contains,密码) OR (subject,contains,sign up) OR (subject,contains,注册) OR (subject,contains,member)"
[/code]
