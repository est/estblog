The science of motivation vs. problem solving

Posted 2009-09-17 | archive

TED.com近期视频：The science of motivation vs. problem solving

“动机”的科学有个很重要的结论：萝卜+大棒不是那么适合21世纪的工作。

As long as the task involved only mechanical skill, bonuses worked as they would be expected: the higher the pay, the better the performance.

But once the task called for "even rudimentary cognitive skill , " a larger reward "let to poorer performance."

"In eight of the nine tasks we examined across the three experiments, higher incentives led to worse performance."

Daniel Pink搞笑的说：这证明了社会主义的优越性，而且是美联储的专家证明，发表在NYtimes上的。LOL

"We find that financial incentives ... can result in a negative impact on overall performance."

Daniel Pink有句话说的很有道理，科学和商业在这一点是矛盾的，商业总是有一种“高回报就有高动机”的assumption，科学反驳了这一点。

还有就是Google的20%时间，也是非常聪明的一个做法

从wikipedia vs Microsoft Encarta也证明一个东西：免费，for fun，说不定就是一个比金钱更有力的动机。

所以有句话说的很好，人们的动机有两个，一个是good reason，一个是real reason。
