Magic Character

Posted 2007-09-02 | archive

Hmm, see what happens when you copy and paste (to anywhere) the following URL

‮/nc.2oy.evitaitini//:ptth

You may also notice that your browser title bar is somehow 'reversed'.

Nice trick, eh?

Wanna know why?

OK, here's the steps,

First type some words in your browser address bar, e.g. http://initiative.yo2.cn/

Then right-click your mouse befor http://

Next select 'Insert Unicode Control Character', and 'RLO Start of right-to-left override'

See your URL has been reversed?

So you can also use this trick to protect your email From been leaked to SPAM, just reverse type your email and add that 'RLO' character before the email address, the email will look normal but reversed!

Note: The first discovery was here in Neowin Forums
