Counter Strike Online (GF/TF/HF) cstrike.nar unpack

Posted 2009-02-21 | archive

Perhaps officially Counter Strike Online cstrike and valve files are extracted by FileSystem_Nar.dll via function CreateInterface, but not an easy way.

Following a Chinese thread I found some Russian hacker in CS.RIN.RU - Steam Underground Community released a tool called NARTool that could easily unpack Gamania\Counter-Strike Online\Data\cstrike.nar and so on.

Just register the forum and grab the tool: NARTool-1.1.rar [112.27 KiB] | NARTool-source.rar [621.79 KiB]

Now trying to play CSOL locally with zombiemod.

ps There's a tool called Crafty that could view Counter Strike maps.
