Gogle Squared出来了

Posted 2009-06-03 | archive

还是比较好玩的一个东西，官方介绍：

Google Squared takes a category and creates a starter 'square' of information, automatically fetching and organizing facts from across the web.

http://www.google.com/squared/search?q=Chinese+ethnics

http://www.google.com/squared/search?q=cylon+models

http://www.google.com/squared/search?q=programming+languages

也支持中文
http://www.google.com/squared/search?q=%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD

http://www.google.com/squared/search?q=chengdu

还是比较有价值的一项服务，但是采集效率、覆盖面和权威程度恐怕短时间是没法有质量保证的。
