Single-process Google Chrome

Posted 2012-09-01 | archive

It's simple, just add a --single-process when launching Google Chrome.

Here's an example of how to modify your desktop "Google Browser" shortcut on Vista:C:\Users\Administrator\AppData\Local\Google\Chrome\Application\chrome.exe --single-process``
