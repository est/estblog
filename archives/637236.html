CS Online Female Character Models for CS 1.6

Posted 2009-02-22 | archive

Haha, just got them working in Counter Strike 1.6

I am not providing downloads for obvious reasons, so I just say the processing steps

First, download Counter Strike Online from tw.csonline.gamania.com

Get CSOTool 1.2 from cs.rin.ru (Thank you Da_FileServer !)

Extract cstrike.nar and grab cstrike/models/players/*.mdl

Decompile & compile the *.mdl using JHLMV

Copy the re-compiled *.mdl files to corresponding CS1.6 directories.

Start Counter Strike 1.6

And before launching game, press ~ for console, and enter mp_consistency 0

Now play with new characters!
