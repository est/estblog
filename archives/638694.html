天朝网监 vs 米国FBI

Posted 2009-04-04 | archive

最近0day把一个本来应该五·一才出来的电影X-Men.Origins.Wolverine.REAL.PROPER.WORKPRINT.XviD-iLG给提前泄漏了。

FBI就直接把整层楼的数据中心给抄了。注意天朝只是封一个网站关一个服务器，或者让IDC负责，FBI是直接拔整个Core IP Networks数据中心的电源。直接后果就是，有可能打911都要收到影响：

Citizen access to Emergency 911 services are being affected, as Core IP's primary client base consists of telephone companies.

这个事情在reddit上也讨论火热

最后文章给出了一句精辟的总结：

If you run a datacenter, please be aware that in our great country , the FBI can come into your place of business at any time and take whatever they want, with no reason.

河蟹是全球性的

Update 2009-4-5 14:23

那么本文观点也无从谈起了。
