5 disturbing features of Google Reader

Posted 2009-07-18 | archive

  • Who the fuck cares how many people like a post? Not say that icon looks really ugly

* Duplicate shares, I am tired of hitting J, J, J, J for the same topic again and again


* What the fuck is comment view? I don't give a fuck to other people's opinion. G Reader should be an **RSS reader** for fuck's sake!
* Why the fuck display a yellow bar saying how many new people are sharing with me?

Just share my GTalk buddy or GMail contacts god damn it!

* What's the difference between _Like_ and _Add star_ seriously? Is there any difference between _Share with Note_ and _Share_ , then _add a comment_?

Frankly I just want some nice RSS reader, where I can spend sometime reading something, with synchronized settings across different computers in different places. What happed to you Google Reader? You used to be cool. Copying Twitter or Friendfeed directly to a RSS reader is just lame

Reading is not social but quite personal, suck it!
