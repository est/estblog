I hate pdf, too

Posted 2008-12-30 | archive

  1. Acrobat Reader is the worst desktop software, ever. Adobe Updater sucks HARD.
    1. It laaaaags your browser, also sometimes .pdf add-ons are a security exploit
    2. Some text / picture are DRM'ed
    3. Scanned magazines converted pdf is the worst kind of all. None-copyable, none-selectable, huge file size, even worse than jpg collections
    4. I have to do horizontal scroll to view a pdf on my mobile. WTF! That's why I read chm ebooks only.
    5. Usability & accessbility issues?
    6. No keyword index, no TOC, sometimes not even searchable.
    7. Scribd's iPaper laaaaags
    8. The content can not be bookmarked, commented and aggregated.
    9. pdf is not interactive
    10. pdf is view-only, it's not even 'readable', not say writable. In a R/W Web, programmable, semantic world today, pdf seemed to be out-dated. Yes it's a nice replacement for Microsoft Word .doc formats, but a terrible digital content format.

In one word, pdf is a stupid technology developed by printing media companies. I hope some hyper text markup (like ReST) could replace that.
